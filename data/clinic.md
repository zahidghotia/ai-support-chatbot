## Frequently asked questions

### Does a root canal hurt?
The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during treatment. Mild soreness for 2 to 3 days afterwards is normal and manageable with paracetamol or ibuprofen.

### How often should I get a cleaning?
Every 6 months for most adults. Patients with gum disease may be advised every 3 to 4 months.

### At what age should a child first see a dentist?
By their first birthday or within 6 months of the first tooth appearing. Dr. Sana Malik handles all pediatric visits.

### Is teeth whitening safe?
Yes, when done professionally. Some temporary sensitivity for 24 to 48 hours is common. Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy or for patients under 16.

### How long do braces take?
Typically 18 to 24 months. Dr. Ayesha Khan does a free initial orthodontic assessment on Saturdays.

### Do you treat patients with diabetes or heart conditions?
Yes. Please inform us of all medical conditions and medications at your first visit so we can plan treatment safely. Patients on blood thinners should tell us before any extraction.

### What should I do if a tooth gets knocked out?
Hold the tooth by the crown (not the root), rinse gently with milk or saline, and try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Call the emergency line immediately. Best results are within 30 to 60 minutes.

### Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?
We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) sedation for an additional 5,000 PKR. Full sedation or general anesthesia is not available at the clinic.
